# B3 Course Rule

This file contains B3-specific rules that override generic retrieval or notation defaults when they conflict.

## Source Families

- B3 has four equal-priority lecture-note source families covering different topic areas:
  - `B3 Atomic Physics (Ewart)` — atomic structure, central field, spin-orbit, LS coupling, Zeeman, and X-rays;
  - `B3 Lasers Notes (2017)` — gain, cavities, laser types, and rate equations;
  - `B3 Quantum Notes` — light-atom interaction, time-dependent perturbation theory, and Rabi physics; and
  - `B3 Radiation Transport (2024)` — radiation transport, optical depth, and Einstein $A/B$ coefficients.
- For any B3 question, check all four locators at the locator-routing stage.
- A B3 question may draw on more than one source family.
- Do not assume that Ewart covers every B3 topic.

## Notation

- For B3 atomic physics, default to Ewart's notation.
- Rydberg energy: $R = 13.6\,\text{eV}$.
- Bohr magneton: $\mu_B = e\hbar/2m_e$.
- Fine-structure constant: $\alpha \approx 1/137$.
- Single-electron angular momenta: $\hat{l}, \hat{s}, \hat{j}$.
- Multi-electron angular momenta: $\hat{L}, \hat{S}, \hat{J}$.
