## Notation And Conventions

- Uses Ewart's B2.III naming for the course, even though the current Oxford label is B3.
- Uses standard atomic notation with `l, s, j` for single-electron angular momentum and `L, S, J` for many-electron states.
- Uses spectroscopic term symbols in the standard `^{2S+1}L_J` form.
- Hyperfine structure is presented in terms of `F = I + J`.
