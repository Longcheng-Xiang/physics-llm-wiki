## What Counts as a Substantive Change

Add an entry when a change affects how a future agent uses, navigates, or maintains the vault. This includes:

- workflow or control-rule changes;
- directory-structure or canonical-path changes;
- additions, removals, or priority changes in lecture-note coverage;
- additions or removals in worked-answer coverage; and
- corrections that materially change future retrieval, calculations, or maintenance.

Do not log ordinary vault use. In particular, do not add entries for files merely read during retrieval, ordinary dialogue answers, read-only checks, isolated wording or formatting fixes, or transient-file cleanup.

## Positive Examples

These are format examples, not real history:

- `YYYY-MM-DD | workflow | Changed the worked-problem gate so a workflow choice is required before retrieval begins.`
- `YYYY-MM-DD | source coverage | Added a canonical lecture-note PDF, its primary text artifact, and its locator.`
- `YYYY-MM-DD | answer coverage | Added a complete worked example with its required audit artifacts.`
- `YYYY-MM-DD | structure | Moved the canonical source inventory and updated every route that points to it.`
- `YYYY-MM-DD | correction | Corrected a locator range that had been routing agents to the wrong derivation.`

## Non-Examples

Do not add entries like these:

- `Read three source files while answering a question.`
- `Answered a conceptual question in the dialogue.`
- `Fixed punctuation or heading spacing in one note.`
- `Deleted a temporary preview, cache file, or operating-system metadata file.`
- `Ran a read-only link, syntax, or secret scan that produced no change.`
